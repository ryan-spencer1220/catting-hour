# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: June 11, 2026**

The Catting Hour ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

## Data We Collect

### Account Information
When you register, we collect your **email address** and **username**. These are used to create and identify your account.

### Location
When you use the map or Nearby features, we access your **precise location** to show cats near you and to tag the location of cats you log. Location is only accessed while the app is in use and is not collected in the background.

### Photos
When you add a cat, you may upload **photos** from your camera or photo library. These are stored securely and associated with the cat's profile.

### User-Generated Content
We store **cat names, notes, temperament, and check-in text** that you submit. This content is visible to other users of the app.

### User IDs
Your account ID is linked to cats you log, check-ins, follows, and other in-app activity.

## How We Use Your Data

- To provide core app features: the cat map, feed, profiles, and nearby search
- To display your username and cats on your public profile
- To allow other users to follow you and see cats you've logged
- To send optional push notifications (only if you enable them)

We do not use your data for advertising, sell it to third parties, or share it with data brokers.

## Third-Party Services

We use **Supabase** (database and authentication) and **Mapbox** (map rendering). These services process data on our behalf under their respective privacy policies. Neither is used for advertising or tracking purposes.

## Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from **Settings → Delete account**, which permanently removes your profile, cats, check-ins, and all associated data.

## Children's Privacy

The Catting Hour is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

## Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.
